# SignalToast — The Catalogue Photo of an Impossible Gadget

A photorealistic studio catalogue photograph of **SignalToast**: a matte-white retro
toaster that toasts your Wi-Fi signal strength onto bread. Two slices pop up with a
Wi-Fi icon visibly toasted onto the crust, a dial on the toaster is labelled
"SIGNAL", and exactly one accessory — a small ceramic butter dish — sits beside it.
No caption, no explanation: the absurd job reads at a glance.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), deterministically.
